Our Story

Built on thirty years
of trust.

OCEANS LLC was founded in 2013 by Daniel Brent and Paul Morrisseau — colleagues who met in Jacksonville when Daniel was serving in the U.S. Navy and later reunited at SRI International in St. Petersburg. They built the company on a simple conviction: federal programs deserve rigorous, objective engineering — not just confident-sounding reports.

Headquartered back where it began in Jacksonville, FL, OCEANS bridges high-level engineering discipline with real-world operational need.
