We started this journal to share the kind of perspective we bring into engagements: how we think about defensible engineering, where rigor pays off, and where it doesn't.

Posts here will be short, structured, and concrete. Less marketing, more notes from the field.

## What you'll find here

- **Method notes** — when a structured T&E approach reveals something a casual review would miss.
- **Architecture observations** — patterns we've seen across federal programs that work, and ones that don't.
- **From the bench** — short pieces on ontologies, mission data, and the COPs that depend on them.

If something here sparks a question, [reach out through the contact form](/contact).
